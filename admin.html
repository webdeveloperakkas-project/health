How post placement works

Main Home Page

Automatic. The newest published post from every category appears in the homepage main slider. It is not a manual placement option.

Latest / Popular

Choose Latest, Popular, Favorites, or any combination. Favorites is reserved for personal picks and affiliate/product recommendations.

Featured Stories / Editor’s Choice

This is a separate editorial selection. Select its checkbox to add a hand-picked article to the homepage Featured Stories section (maximum 20).

Automatic ranking by real article views after live view tracking is connected.
